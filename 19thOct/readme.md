<h2>Topics Learned</h2>

<h3>1. Introduction to Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning</h3>

<p><b>What is Machine Learning?</b> Learned the basic concept of Machine Learning and how it enables systems to learn patterns from data and make predictions or decisions.</p>

<p><b>AI vs. ML vs. DL:</b> Studied the relationship and differences between Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), and Deep Learning (DL).</p>

<h3>2. Machine Learning</h3>

<p><b>Detailed Understanding of Machine Learning:</b> Studied how machine learning works, how models learn from data, and how they are used to solve different problems.</p>

<p><b>Types of Machine Learning:</b> Learned the main categories of machine learning:</p>

<ul>
<li><b>Supervised Learning:</b> Learns from labelled data to make predictions.</li>
<li><b>Unsupervised Learning:</b> Identifies patterns and structures in unlabelled data.</li>
<li><b>Semi-Supervised Learning:</b> Uses a combination of labelled and unlabelled data.</li>
<li><b>Reinforcement Learning:</b> Learns through interactions with an environment using rewards and penalties.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Machine Learning Algorithms:</b> Explored an overview of different algorithms used in machine learning, including regression, classification, clustering, and dimensionality reduction techniques.</p>

<h3>3. Deep Learning</h3>

<p><b>Types of Deep Learning Models:</b> Learned about different deep learning architectures and their applications.</p>

<p><b>Deep Learning Algorithms:</b> Studied an overview of common approaches, including Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs), Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs), and Transformers.</p>

<h3>4. Types of Artificial Intelligence</h3>

<p><b>Based on Capabilities:</b> Learned about the different categories of AI based on their capabilities.</p>

<ul>
<li><b>Narrow AI (ANI):</b> Designed to perform specific tasks.</li>
<li><b>Artificial General Intelligence (AGI):</b> A theoretical form of AI capable of performing a broad range of intellectual tasks.</li>
<li><b>Artificial Superintelligence (ASI):</b> A hypothetical form of AI that surpasses human intelligence across many areas.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Classification of AI Systems by Techniques:</b> Explored different approaches used to classify AI systems, including rule-based systems, machine learning-based systems, and deep learning-based systems.</p>

<h3>5. Basic Machine Learning Terminology</h3>

<p>Learned the fundamental terminology used in machine learning, including datasets, features, labels, samples, models, training, testing, validation, predictions, parameters, hyperparameters, loss functions, and evaluation metrics.</p>
